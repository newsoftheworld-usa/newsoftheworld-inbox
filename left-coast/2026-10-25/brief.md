- On Oct. 13 U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes halted tree-cutting at the D.C. course until Oct. 26 after at least 400 trees were cut over the holiday weekend; the government must give three business days' written advance notice before irreversible changes. https://www.ksat.com/news/politics/2026/10/13/federal-judge-orders-trump-administration-to-halt-work-on-historic-dc-golf-course-until-oct-26/
- Reyes at the hearing: "What has happened has cost the government all credibility with me." She wrote officials rushed because "President Trump demands haste on his redesign." (same URL)
- Trump, in a June post, said the course would host "Major Golf Tournaments." (same URL)
- Suit brought by the DC Preservation League and two local golfers. (same URL)
