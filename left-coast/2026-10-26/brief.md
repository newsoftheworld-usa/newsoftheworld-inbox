# Left Coast Report — research brief, 2026-10-26

## 1. Bishops regret the White House prayer photo with Trump
- Milwaukee Archbishop Jeffrey Grob and Des Moines Bishop William Joensen said they regret attending a White House meeting with President Trump on Thursday, Oct. 22; twelve bishops attended. https://abcnews.com/Politics/wireStory/catholic-bishops-regret-white-house-prayer-photo-trump-137557990
- The White House publicized photos and a video of the group praying, less than two weeks before the midterms. https://abcnews.com/Politics/wireStory/catholic-bishops-regret-white-house-prayer-photo-trump-137557990
- Asked whether it had the bishops' consent, the White House press office declined to answer. https://abcnews.com/Politics/wireStory/catholic-bishops-regret-white-house-prayer-photo-trump-137557990
- Joensen: "I wish I had been more shrewd and less simple, like those who exploited our meeting for partisan political purpose" (https://abcnews.com/Politics/wireStory/catholic-bishops-regret-white-house-prayer-photo-trump-137557990)
- Grob: "The meeting was in no way an endorsement of the president, his policies, or his party." (same URL)
- White House spokeswoman Taylor Rogers: "There has never been a greater president for Catholic Americans than President Trump, and his strong record proves it." (same URL)
- First Amendment: "Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof;" https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights-transcript

## 2. Trump-appointed judge doubts DOJ's California ballot demand
- The Justice Department asked the 9th Circuit to force California to set aside as many as 20,000 ballots tied to suspected noncitizens; a 105-minute emergency hearing took place Sunday. https://www.votebeat.org/national/2026/10/26/trump-california-noncitizens-ballots-lawsuit-midterm/
- Panel: Mark Bennett (Trump appointee), Gabriel Sanchez (Biden appointee), Patrick Bumatay (Trump appointee); no ruling yet. https://www.foxnews.com/politics/ninth-circuit-questions-trump-doj-emergency-request-set-aside-20000-california-mail-ballots
- Bennett: "I don't see how we have jurisdiction." https://www.votebeat.org/national/2026/10/26/trump-california-noncitizens-ballots-lawsuit-midterm/
- Bumatay seemed more sympathetic to DOJ. https://www.votebeat.org/national/2026/10/26/trump-california-noncitizens-ballots-lawsuit-midterm/
- California officials say the list is "deeply flawed." (same Votebeat URL)
