# Research brief — 2026-10-21

## 1. Comer says Omar-linked winery was "shut down because of fraud"
- House Oversight Chairman James Comer (R-Ky.) said on Fox News' "Sunday Night in America" that the California winery eStCru, co-founded in 2020 by Tim Mynett, Rep. Ilhan Omar's husband, was shut down because of fraud. (https://www.foxnews.com/politics/winery-owned-ilhan-omars-husband-shuttered-due-fraud-james-comer-says)
- Comer alleges the winery sold empty barrels to investors as "wine to be aged"; the winery closed in April 2026. (same URL)
- Omar's 2023 disclosure listed her eStCru stake at $15,001–$50,000; her 2024 disclosure listed $1,000,001–$5,000,000. She previously blamed an accounting error. (same URL)
- Lawyers for Mynett and his partner told the Minnesota Reformer that any suggestion of deliberate fraud would be "false and defamatory." These are allegations by Comer, not findings. (same URL)
- House Oversight referred the matter to the House Ethics Committee months ago; Ethics has not yet taken up the case. (same URL)
- Quote — Comer: "That winery has, according to the press, been shut down because of fraud." (same URL)
- Quote — Comer: "there are multiple fronts where you could get a search warrant issued." (same URL)

## 2. Blakeman rides the NYC subway, hammers Hochul and Mamdani
- Bruce Blakeman, Republican candidate for New York governor and Nassau County executive, campaigned by riding the NYC subway. (https://www.foxnews.com/politics/bruce-blakeman-hammers-dem-gov-hochul-mamdani-subway-safety-nyc-campaign-ride)
- Hochul vetoed a two-person-crew (operator and conductor) bill on Dec. 5, 2025, arguing it would restrict MTA modernization and cost up to $10 million a year. (same URL)
- Blakeman wants more police, better lighting, more conductors and mental-health help in the system, and opposes congestion pricing. (same URL)
- Quote — Blakeman: "the most pro-criminal governor in the United States of America." (same URL)
- Quote — Blakeman: "The crime in the subways has gone up tremendously under Kathy Hochul and Zohran Mamdani." (same URL)
- Quote — Blakeman: "People feel less safe in every community that I go to in New York State." (same URL)

## 3. Trump reportedly floats turning the press room back into a pool
- The New York Times reported Oct. 20 that Trump is weighing restoring the White House briefing room to its original state as a swimming pool, per the Washington Examiner. (https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/white-house/4773355/trump-white-house-press-room-swimming-pool/)
- He reportedly raised it with Karoline Leavitt, who left as press secretary in August 2026, and told her to remove reporters from the room. (same URL)
- The Examiner piece is secondhand: no direct quotes from Trump or Leavitt and no White House response. Shorts attribute everything to the Times report. (same URL)
