## 1. Obama Presidential Center cost balloons to about $1 billion
- The Obama Foundation projects the Obama Presidential Center in Chicago's Jackson Park will cost about $1 billion, making it the costliest U.S. presidential center to date. https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/politics/4766970/obama-president-campus-cost-balloon-1-billion-top-expensive-presidential-center/
- Initial estimates were as low as $300 million; $500 million in 2017; $700 million in construction costs in Aug. 2021; $850 million confirmed in Sept. 2025. (same URL)
- The George W. Bush Presidential Library, at $327 million, is the next most expensive. (same URL)
- Delays cited: a court fight with local residents over Jackson Park, its National Register of Historic Places listing, and further lawsuits. (same URL)
- Quote, Emily Bittner, Obama Foundation VP of communications: "anticipate[s] our total cost for constructing the campus is approximately $1 billion." She said funding was "all raised in private contributions." (same URL)
