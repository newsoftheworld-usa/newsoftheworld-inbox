## 1. Judge tosses John Brennan's lawsuit against Trump
Source for all bullets: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/biden-judge-rejects-obama-cia-chiefs-legal-bid-against-trump-over-one-key-problem (Fox News, Oct 16, 2026)
- Judge Jia Cobb, a Biden appointee, dismissed former CIA Director John Brennan's lawsuit against Trump and senior officials, ruling he lacked standing.
- She found his worry about destroyed evidence "speculative" and called allegations the administration targets him "deeply concerning."
- Brennan is a target in two federal investigations (three grand jury inquiries, two in S.D. Florida, one in D.C.); he has not been charged.
- One probe covers alleged conspiracy to violate Trump's civil rights over Russia-related investigations; another covers alleged false statements in 2023 congressional testimony about the Steele dossier.
- The suit was filed July 1, 2026; the 46-page complaint cited "the president's retribution agenda."
- Quote, Judge Cobb: "a series of dominoes must fall, and Defendants' deletion of records is just one of those dominoes."
- Quote, Brennan's lawyers: "Director Brennan will continue to vigorously defend himself against these baseless investigations."
