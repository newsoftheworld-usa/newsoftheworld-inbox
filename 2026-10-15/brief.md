## 3. Trump-nominated judge keeps CNN, MS NOW, Politico reporters in the White House
- U.S. District Judge Timothy Kelly, nominated by Trump, issued a preliminary injunction on Tuesday, Oct. 13, extending the block on the administration barring reporters from CNN, MS NOW and Politico. https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/judge-extends-order-blocking-trump-from-banning-cnn-ms-now-and-politico-reporters-from-white-house
- He found the outlets likely to succeed on First Amendment and due process claims, and rejected a request to pause his ruling pending appeal. (same PBS URL)
- The White House gave no advance warning before Trump's Sept. 18 social media post announcing the bans, which called the outlets' reporting "fake news." 78 reporters were affected. (same PBS URL)
- Kelly found the national security arguments came after the fact and were unsupported. (same PBS URL)
- Quote, Judge Kelly: "The ban threatens to cut off all Plaintiffs' access to the White House press area." (same PBS URL)
- Quote, DOJ lawyers: "access to the White House is a privilege — not a right." (same PBS URL)
- Quote, attorney Theodore Boutrous Jr.: "he blatantly and explicitly violated due process and the First Amendment." (same PBS URL)
